A callback is a known outcome when it passed, failed natively, is a verified refusal (Mermaid recognises the refused source as a diagram) or is a confirmed absence (a recorded review confirmed the capability assessment). Every other scored callback is unassessed: it could still pass or fail, which is why the league table shows a possible rank. Across all candidates 514 callbacks are unassessed. Each has an owner who can close it:

- **Trident: binding or view**: closed by a reviewed Trident binding or view that reads what the candidate exposes.

- **Trident: defect**: closed by fixing the Trident error.
- **Evidence: unreviewed assessment**: closed by a review entry that confirms or disproves the capability assessment.
- **Evidence: unrecognised refusal**: closed by better evidence only. Mermaid's detector does not recognise the refused source either, or no refused source was recorded, so the callback may legitimately stay unassessed.

An adapter limitation that is neither a file that did not load nor a Trident error counts as binding or view work.
