# Benchmark overview

Trident's public workloads are community output admission and API/CLI costs, plus the portable authored family/scaling campaign. Use [the benchmark guide](BENCHMARKS.md) to decide whether a workload answers your question.

The remaining inherited workloads—Dave, authored fidelity, upstream assertions, startup, release/peer, editor and source diagnostics—remain callable through [the source adapter](adapters/agentic/README.md). Their fixed-engine or source dependencies are explicit. They are not all independent benchmarks and do not make every catalog project benchmark-ready.

[Methodology](METHODOLOGY.md) defines the common rules. [Measurement rules](MEASUREMENT.md) explain their limits. [Results](results/README.md) describes collecting fresh evidence; no old score is presented as current.
